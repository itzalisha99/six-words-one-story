One complete story in exactly six words (37 characters):

> The letter arrived after the funeral.

A letter written in time, arriving too late — the apology, the love,
the thing left unsaid, all of it landing after the funeral. The story is
what the reader fills in: who wrote it, what it said, what was lost.

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes that
story to `/output/entry.txt`.
